Tell GPT to follow these rules:

- مبلغ: numbers only, without thousands separators. Use one unit throughout: ریال or تومان.
- تاریخ: Jalali date in YYYY/MM/DD format.
- ساعت: 24-hour time, HH:mm:ss.
- کد پیگیری: preserve the original code and leading zeros.
- نوع: برداشت for expenses; واریز for income.
- Don’t invent missing details. Leave missing times or tracking codes blank.

When importing, select UTF-8, Comma, Jalali, Year/month/day, and IRR for ریال or IRT for تومان.

If your workspace still contains USD sample data, back it up and clear it in Settings before importing rials or tomans.
